# A plate is planned through one path, and only what is ahead holds stock

- EVERY write that puts a dish on a plate MUST go through `commitPlan`, whether it is a week from the
  tunnel or one plate from a recipe screen. A second writer of `plannedMeals` MUST NOT exist.
- `syncMealLines` MUST be the only writer of `plannedMealIngredients` and of the grocery delta a plate
  implies. Re-portioning, removing and replacing a plate MUST be expressed as a call to it, never as
  a second arithmetic over the ledger.
- Quantities MUST be recomputed server-side at commit time against the cellar as it stands. The
  client sends choices — which dish, which day, which slot, how many — and MUST NOT send amounts.
- A plate MUST count as holding stock only while `date >= today` and `status === "planned"`. Nothing
  decrements the cellar yet, so a past plate MUST NOT reserve anything, or stock is held forever.
- A plate MUST NOT land on a day before `today`, and a week generated by the tunnel MUST start after
  the last planned day and never on `today`: the earliest plannable day is one there is time to shop
  for. A single plate from a recipe screen MAY land on `today`.
- A plate already on a day and slot MUST be replaced by dropping it first, so its claims are given
  back before the new plate's are weighed. A cooked plate MUST NOT be replaced.
- `mealPlans.seed` MUST be absent on a plan no shuffle drew. `0` is a seed, and a reroll would
  faithfully reproduce a week nobody generated.
- Reads MUST draw `plannedMeals` by date, never `mealPlans`: a plan's creation order stops matching
  the calendar the moment a plate is planned outside the tunnel.

## Why

The planning slice landed in seven steps, and the seventh added a second caller of the write path
rather than a second write path: the recipe screen's "add to my list" is `commitPlan` with one
plate. Everything that makes the feature true — the cellar weighed at commit time, the ledger, the
grocery merge, the reservation of stock by meals still ahead — lives once, and a second copy would
agree with the first only until somebody changed one of them. The rules here are what that single
path settled; the narrative of how the week is generated and scored is in
[../ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

Reservations by future plates only is the rule most likely to be "fixed" by someone who has not
read this: counting every planned plate looks more complete, and holds a household's rice hostage
to a dinner it ate last month.
